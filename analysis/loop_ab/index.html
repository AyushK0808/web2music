Can you hear where the music repeats?

Twenty short pairs of clips. In each pair, one clip contains a repeat and the other does not. Your job is to say which. About 15 minutes.

There is no sign-up and nothing is stored about you. At the end you get your score.

You need headphones. The thing you are listening for is not audible on laptop or phone speakers, and answers from speakers would make the results meaningless rather than merely noisy.

Headphone check

Three tones will play. One of them is quieter than the other two. Which one?

Before you start

  1. You will hear 20 pairs of short instrumental clips generated by a research system.
  2. Nothing identifying is collected. No account, no email, no cookies beyond your own progress so you can close the tab and come back.
  3. You can stop at any point; partial answers are still useful and are only used if you choose to submit them.
  4. Answers are used in aggregate in an academic paper about music generation.
