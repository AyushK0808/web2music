S2 annotation

Before you start

Do not discuss any page with another annotator until everyone has submitted. Agreement produced by conversation inflates Krippendorff's α, and α is the ceiling every accuracy number in the paper is measured against. An inflated ceiling is worse than a low one, because it silently raises the bar the classifier is judged by and nobody can see it.

You will see the text the extension extracted from each page — not the live page. That is deliberate: you are labelling the same thing the classifier sees.

Roughly 45 seconds per page. Take a break every 30–40 pages; label quality falls off noticeably after about an hour, and your progress is saved automatically.

What the three labels mean

Category — what the page is about. One of thirteen. If two fit, pick the one a reader would name first.

Mood — framed as a preference, not a fact: which of these eleven moods would you least object to hearing while reading this page? There is no correct answer, and that is the point. Do not try to guess what the system would say.

Sensitive — would auto-playing any music here be an intrusion? Crisis and harm content, not merely serious or sad content. A history article about a famine is not sensitive; a page about someone's suicidal thoughts is.
